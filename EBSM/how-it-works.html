How ReSource works

One contractor’s surplus is another buyer’s savings.

  1. List your surplus

    Contractors take a photo and list leftover building materials like lumber, tile, and drywall.

  2. Schedule a pickup

    ReSource picks up the materials, grades them for quality, and stores them at a local hub.

  3. Shop graded materials

    Buyers browse available inventory and purchase discounted materials for their next project.
