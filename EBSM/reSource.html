About ReSource

Good materials deserve another job.

Leftover lumber, tile, and drywall still have value, but reselling them takes time and coordinating pickup adds another task to the job. ReSource gives contractors a way to recover some of that value and helps local renovators and DIY buyers find discounted supplies.
